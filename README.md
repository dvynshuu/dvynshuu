### About

Most modern software is optimized for attention extraction and continuous cloud dependency. I'm interested in the inverse: building tools that respect the human mind, encrypt sensitive thoughts by default, and feel quiet, fast, and durable.

My work sits at the intersection of:
- **Zero-knowledge privacy & encryption** — giving people complete sovereignty over their inner world and personal data.
- **Local-first architecture** — offline-capable, zero-latency software powered by client databases (IndexedDB/Dexie) rather than renting your thoughts back from a cloud server.
- **Spatial computing & graphics on the web** — using Three.js and WebGL to make complex systems tangible and visually intuitive.

### Principles I Build By

1. **Privacy is an architectural decision, not a settings toggle.**  
   If an application holds someone's thoughts, emotions, or notes, the architecture should make unauthorized access mathematically impossible — not just legally prohibited.

2. **Software should calm the nervous system.**  
   Modern digital spaces are saturated with notifications, red badges, and infinite loops. I design software with high craft, fast feedback loops, and quiet interfaces that let people return to their lives with greater clarity.

3. **Local-first by conviction.**  
   Your device is powerful enough to run your software. Whenever possible, compute and persist data at the edge before considering the cloud.
